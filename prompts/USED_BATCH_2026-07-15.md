## Naming convention

```
YYYY-MM-DD_<short_theme_slug>.jpg
```

- Date = **Asia/Kolkata** calendar day you want the rotator to prefer.  
- Theme slug = snake_case, short, descriptive.  
- Prefer JPG/PNG; avoid spaces in filenames.
